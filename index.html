Financial Modeling Projects

A selection of Excel-based financial modeling projects demonstrating my understanding of corporate valuation, cost of capital, and financial analysis. These projects apply financial concepts to practical business decision-making.

PROJECT / 01 · FINANCIAL VALUATION

Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Model

Developed an Excel-based Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model to estimate a company's enterprise value using projected future cash flows. The model incorporates revenue growth assumptions, operating projections, free cash flow calculations, and terminal value. Future cash flows are discounted using the weighted average cost of capital to estimate the present value of the business.

  • Five-year financial projections
  • Free cash flow forecasting
  • Terminal value estimation
  • Enterprise valuation using WACC

PROJECT / 02 · COST OF CAPITAL

Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) Calculator

Created an Excel-based Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) calculator to evaluate a company's overall cost of financing. The model incorporates the company's capital structure, cost of equity, cost of debt, and corporate tax rate to calculate WACC. This analysis demonstrates how financing decisions influence a company's required rate of return and investment valuation.

  • Equity and debt financing weights
  • Cost of equity and cost of debt
  • Corporate tax adjustments
  • Weighted average cost of capital
